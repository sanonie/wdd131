Site Plan Document

Site Name

EcoTrail Adventures

I chose this name because it reflects the purpose of the project: exploring nature, promoting sustainable outdoor activities, and offering eco‑friendly travel information. The name is memorable and aligns with the theme of environmental awareness.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide visitors with information about eco‑friendly hiking trails, guided tours, outdoor safety, and environmental education. The site will help users discover new places to explore while learning how to minimize their environmental impact.

Scenarios

  • “What beginner‑friendly hiking trails are available near me?”
  • “How can I prepare for my first eco‑friendly outdoor adventure?”

Color Schema

The color palette is inspired by nature and sustainability.

#1B5E20 – Dark Forest Green: Used for headings and accents.

#4CAF50 – Accent Green: Used for highlights and borders.

#F4F7F5 – Light Sage: Used for the background.

Typography

  • Montserrat – Used for headings.
  • Roboto – Used for body text.
